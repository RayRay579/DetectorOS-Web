# Getting Started with DetectorOS Welcome to DetectorOS — your field intelligence platform for metal detecting, relic hunting, treasure hunting, and outdoor exploration. This guide will walk you through your first hunt from start to finish. --- # Step 1: Create Your Account 📱 Open DetectorOS 1. Tap **Create Account** 2. Enter your email address 3. Create a secure password 4. Verify your email if prompted 5. Log in Once logged in, you'll be taken to the DetectorOS dashboard. --- # Step 2: Explore the Dashboard 🏠 The Dashboard is your command center. From here you can access: 🗺️ Maps 📍 Routes 📸 Find Logger 👥 Team Operations ⚡ DetectorOS Pro Take a few minutes to familiarize yourself with the layout before heading into the field. --- # Step 3: Plan Your Hunt 🗺️ Open the Map screen. Before starting a hunt: 1. Identify your hunt location 2. Review weather conditions 3. Check access permissions 4. Plan your search pattern Tip: Grid-style searches often produce more consistent results than random wandering. --- # Step 4: Start Tracking a Route 📍 Open Hunt Routes 1. Tap **Start Route** 2. Begin your hunt 3. DetectorOS will record your path 4. Continue detecting normally Benefits: ✅ Avoid searching the same area twice ✅ Review productive locations ✅ Build a history of successful hunts --- # Step 5: Log a Find 📸 When you recover an item: 1. Open Find Logger 2. Tap **Add Find** 3. Take a photo 4. Add notes 5. Save GPS location 6. Tap Save Examples: 🪙 Coin 💍 Ring 🔘 Button ⚔️ Relic 🥇 Jewelry 📦 Miscellaneous Item Every find becomes part of your permanent hunt history. --- # Step 6: Review Your Hunt After your session: 1. Open Hunt Routes 2. Review your path 3. Open Find Logger 4. Review discoveries 5. Add additional notes if needed This helps identify productive locations and patterns. --- # Step 7: Organize Your Finds DetectorOS allows you to build a searchable archive of your discoveries. Use notes to record: 📅 Date Found 📍 Location 📝 Historical Information 🏷️ Item Identification Over time this becomes your personal detecting journal. --- # Step 8: Use Team Operations 👥 Team Operations helps organize group hunts. You can: • Coordinate with other detectorists • Share activity information • Manage organized hunt events Perfect for clubs, partnerships, and group outings. --- # Step 9: Upgrade to DetectorOS Pro ⚡ DetectorOS Pro unlocks advanced tools: ✅ Unlimited Finds ✅ Unlimited Routes ✅ Advanced Weather Intelligence ✅ Cloud Sync ✅ Export Tools ✅ Premium Field Operations Current Subscription: DetectorOS Pro Monthly $3.99/month --- # Field Safety Tips Always: ✅ Obtain permission before detecting ✅ Respect private property ✅ Follow local laws and regulations ✅ Fill holes properly ✅ Leave sites cleaner than you found them ✅ Monitor weather conditions --- # Need Help?
