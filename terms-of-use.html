About DetectorOS

DetectorOS is a field intelligence app built for metal detectorists, relic hunters, treasure seekers, and outdoor explorers. The app helps users plan hunts, track routes, log finds, monitor weather, and organize detecting sessions.

Subscriptions

DetectorOS Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription available through Apple’s App Store. DetectorOS Pro unlocks premium features such as unlimited finds, unlimited routes, advanced weather intelligence, cloud sync, team operations, and export tools.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID account settings.
