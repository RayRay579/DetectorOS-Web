DetectorOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

DetectorOS ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

DetectorOS may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, profile details, and authentication credentials
  • Location Data: Precise GPS coordinates for mapping, hunt tracking, and route features
  • User-Generated Content: Find logs, routes, notes, photos, and hunt history
  • Subscription Data: Purchase status, subscription tier, and billing information (processed through Apple App Store and RevenueCat)
  • Diagnostic Data: App crash logs, performance metrics, and usage statistics to improve stability and functionality
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Providing core app functionality and features
  • Synchronizing hunt data and user content across devices
  • Processing subscriptions and enabling Pro features
  • Improving app performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our Terms of Use

Third-Party Services

DetectorOS uses trusted third-party services that may process limited user data necessary for app functionality:

  • Apple App Store - App distribution and in-app purchases
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management and billing
  • Supabase - Database and backend services
  • Expo / EAS - App development and hosting infrastructure

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies to understand how they handle your data.

Data Sharing

DetectorOS is committed to protecting your privacy:

  • We do not sell user data to advertisers or data brokers
  • We do not share personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide app services
  • We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication and password policies
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

While we strive to protect your information, no security system is completely impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your Privacy Choices

  • Location Access: You can disable location services through your device settings at any time. Note that this may limit app functionality
  • Account Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Data Access: You may request to see what personal data we have collected about you
  • Opt-Out: You can disable push notifications and diagnostic data sharing in app settings

Children's Privacy

DetectorOS is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

International Users

If you are located outside the United States, your data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States and other countries. By using DetectorOS, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence, which may have data protection rules different from those of your country.
